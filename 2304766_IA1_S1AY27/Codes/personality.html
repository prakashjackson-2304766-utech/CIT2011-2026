Critical Essay: LLMs as a Tool, Not a Crutch

Large Language Models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot have transformed computing education. However, relying on them to write core logic prevents students from developing essential problem-solving neural pathways. In my studies, LLMs serve as interactive tutors for clarifying complex syntax or reviewing logic errors, ensuring that technical mastery remains my own.
