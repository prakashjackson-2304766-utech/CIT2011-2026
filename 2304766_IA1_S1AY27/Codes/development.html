Reflection: 7 Cures for a Lean Purse

George S. Clason's The Richest Man in Babylon outlines fundamental financial principles. The first cure, "Start thy purse to fattening," emphasizes saving at least 10% of all earnings. As a student, applying this habit builds discipline and financial security before entering the workforce full-time.

Reflection: 5 Laws of Gold

The 5 Laws of Gold highlight that wealth grows for those who invest wisely and seek advice from competent experts. This principle directly applies to technology careers: investing time in core competencies yields long-term career growth.
